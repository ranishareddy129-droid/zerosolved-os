Overview
The whole case on one page.
Patients and employers struggle to understand healthcare costs due to opaque and complex hospital price files. These files, mandated for publication, are technically complete but intentionally obscure, making meaningful comparison impossible for the average consumer. This persists because hospitals have little incentive to truly improve transparency when it could lead to lost revenue.
researcher note · [modeled]
A platform that ingests, standardizes, and simplifies hospital price data will enable consumers and employers to make informed decisions, driving down healthcare costs.
The case in 30 seconds
