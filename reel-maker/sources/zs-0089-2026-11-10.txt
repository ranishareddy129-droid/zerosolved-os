Overview
The whole case on one page.
Private outpatient clinics lose 12% to 18% of available clinical hours to unannounced missed appointments and late cancellations. Traditional reminder tools fail because they verify attendance days in advance without resolving day-of logistical friction or autonomously refilling empty slots when cancellations inevitably happen.
researcher note · [modeled]
Independent outpatient practices will pay $199 to $399 monthly per clinic for an autonomous standby queue that backfills late cancellations within 20 minutes without front-desk manual outreach; downgrade if EHR API gatekeeping prevents real-time calendar read/write access.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Practice managers and lead clinicians in private allied health, psychotherapy, dental, and outpatient specialty practices with 2 to 10 providers.
the bleed Lost gross revenue per clinical provider every year due to unrecovered late cancellations
the gap Incumbent patient communication platforms send one-way SMS confirmations 48 hours prior, whereas clinics need automated, real-time micro-deposit enforcement paired with zero-click waitlist dispatch when a slot opens within two hours of scheduled time.
the wedge A webhook-driven SMS and WhatsApp standby bot that connects to Cliniko or SimplePractice to fill same-day cancellations from an active client waitlist in under 15 minutes.
the endgame The autonomous yield-management and dynamic capacity engine powering outpatient healthcare scheduling globally.
the window 24-36 months, as modern practice management systems expand FHIR-compliant open APIs and patients expect instant chat-based scheduling.
